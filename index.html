Welcome

I’m Rebecca, and Root & Stem is where I share my yoga classes: practices that start from a steady base and grow from there.

I’ve practiced yoga for over 20 years, and for the last 7 I’ve studied handstands in depth with Virgile Peyramaure. In 2026 I completed my 200-hour teacher training with Jason Crandell.

My teaching follows three paths:

  • Seasonal yoga. Sequences shaped by the time of year, starting with the Autumn Yoga Sequence.
  • Handbalancing for beginners. The foundations of arm balances and handstands, broken into steps you can practice at home.
  • Yoga beyond the mat. Bringing ideas from yoga philosophy, like non-attachment, into working life, for more ease, happiness and connection with the people around you. It’s shaped by my own career as a software engineer.

I'm brand new to teaching, and building content first round seasonal yoga. More is on the way!
